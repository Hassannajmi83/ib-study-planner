Subject Guide

History

History is a argument + evidence + historiography subject. High marks come from clear structure and balanced analysis.

High score rule: Always answer the question directly and maintain argument focus throughout.

Assessment Structure

Structure depends on SL/HL and syllabus version.

Paper 1

Source analysis.

  • Origin, Purpose, Content, Value, Limitation.
  • Use source evidence directly.
  • Link to context.

Paper 2

Essay responses.

  • Clear thesis statement.
  • Use specific historical evidence.
  • Balance multiple perspectives.

Paper 3 (HL)

Depth essays.

  • Detailed knowledge.
  • Historiographical references.
  • Strong evaluation.

Best Study Method (History)

  • Create thematic timelines.
  • Practice essay writing weekly.
  • Memorize key statistics/dates.
  • Review historiographical debates.
History mistake pattern: Writing narrative without argument.

Essay Structure Template

  • Introduction: Direct thesis answering question.
  • Body paragraphs: Claim → Evidence → Analysis.
  • Counter-argument: Alternative interpretation.
  • Conclusion: Clear judgement.
Use linking phrases: “This suggests…”, “However…”, “On balance…”

Common Mark Traps (History)

Trap 1: No argument

Always state and defend a clear thesis.

Trap 2: Weak evidence

Use specific names, dates, and events.

Trap 3: Ignoring counterpoints

Include alternative interpretations for balance.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I form a clear thesis quickly?
  • Do I support claims with evidence?
  • Do I evaluate alternative perspectives?
  • Is my conclusion decisive?
