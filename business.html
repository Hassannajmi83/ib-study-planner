Subject Guide

Business Management

Business is a definition + structure + application subject. Marks come from using correct terminology and applying it to case studies.

High score rule: Always define first, then apply to the case.

Assessment Structure

Structure depends on SL/HL and syllabus version.

Paper 1

Case study analysis.

  • Define business concepts.
  • Apply directly to the case.
  • Use business terminology accurately.

Paper 2

Structured responses.

  • Short answers (definitions + explanation).
  • Long responses with evaluation.

Internal Assessment

Business report.

  • Clear research question.
  • Use business tools (SWOT, Ansoff, etc.).
  • Evidence-based recommendations.

Best Study Method (Business)

  • Memorize key definitions.
  • Practice case-based questions.
  • Use real-world examples.
  • Review evaluation techniques weekly.
Business mistake pattern: Students forget to apply theory directly to the case.

Answer Structure Template

  • Define key term.
  • Explain theory clearly.
  • Apply directly to the case.
  • Evaluate (advantages + disadvantages).
  • Conclusion with recommendation.
Always reference the case study data in your answer.

Common Mark Traps (Business)

Trap 1: No application

Always link answer to the case context.

Trap 2: Weak evaluation

Give balanced pros and cons before judgement.

Trap 3: Missing terminology

Use precise business vocabulary.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I define core business terms?
  • Do I apply theory directly to case data?
  • Do I evaluate with balanced judgement?
  • Do I structure answers clearly?
