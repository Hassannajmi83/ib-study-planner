Study Planner

Time management only. Enter your hours per day, days until the exam, and your subjects. The plan shifts from content toward past papers as the exam gets closer.

Add a number after a comma to say how much a subject needs. 1 is normal, 5 is struggling. No number means 1.

Rules
  1. Weaker subjects come up more often, but stay spread out
  2. Early days: mostly content. Later days: mostly past papers
  3. Short, consistent sessions win
  4. Your inputs are saved on this device
