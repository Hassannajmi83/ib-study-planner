How to use your plan (so it actually works)

  • Weekly loop: Topic sets → mixed sets → timed set → fix mistakes → repeat.
  • Timed every week: at least 1 “mini-paper” under time (even if short).
  • Error rule: every wrong question becomes a one-line rule (your personal marks scheme).
  • Don’t rewrite notes: if you’re “studying” but not answering questions, it’s usually fake progress.
