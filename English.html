Subject Guide

English

English is a thesis + structure + analysis subject. High marks come from clear arguments and close analysis of language.

High score rule: Every paragraph must link back to your thesis.

Assessment Structure

Structure depends on Language & Literature or Literature, and SL/HL.

Paper 1

Textual analysis.

  • Clear thesis in introduction.
  • Analyse techniques (tone, imagery, structure).
  • Link analysis to overall meaning.

Paper 2

Comparative essay.

  • Compare themes/techniques across works.
  • Balanced structure.
  • Strong conclusion.

IO (Internal Oral)

Global issue analysis.

  • Clear global issue.
  • Extract analysis.
  • Structured delivery.

Best Study Method (English)

  • Practice timed essays weekly.
  • Memorize key quotes.
  • Build theme + technique tables.
  • Analyse model essays.
English mistake pattern: Too much summary, not enough analysis.

Essay Structure Template

  • Introduction: clear thesis + argument roadmap.
  • Body paragraph: Point → Evidence → Analysis → Link.
  • Comparison: Integrate both texts.
  • Conclusion: Reinforce thesis clearly.
Use analytical verbs: “suggests”, “implies”, “highlights”, “reinforces”.

Common Mark Traps (English)

Trap 1: Summary

Don’t retell the story — analyse language and meaning.

Trap 2: Weak thesis

State a clear argument, not a topic statement.

Trap 3: No comparison

Always connect both texts clearly in Paper 2.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I write a clear thesis in 5 minutes?
  • Do my paragraphs analyse language?
  • Do I integrate quotes smoothly?
  • Do I link back to the question?
