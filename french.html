Subject Guide

French

French is a structure + vocabulary + fluency subject. High marks come from correct grammar and clear organization.

High score rule: Prioritize grammar accuracy over complex vocabulary you can’t control.

Assessment Structure

Structure depends on SL/HL and syllabus version.

Paper 1

Writing.

  • Choose correct text type.
  • Follow required format.
  • Use varied vocabulary and tenses.

Paper 2

Listening + Reading.

  • Identify key words.
  • Answer precisely (no extra words).
  • Practice under time pressure.

Oral

Speaking assessment.

  • Structured responses.
  • Clear pronunciation.
  • Use linking phrases.

Best Study Method (French)

  • Practice writing weekly.
  • Review grammar rules regularly.
  • Build vocabulary lists by theme.
  • Listen to French media daily.
French mistake pattern: Students use advanced words but incorrect grammar.

Writing Structure Template

  • Opening greeting (if required).
  • Introduction paragraph.
  • Main body (2–3 paragraphs).
  • Conclusion + closing phrase.
Always include connectives: “Cependant”, “En revanche”, “De plus”.

Common Mark Traps (French)

Trap 1: Wrong format

Follow required text type structure strictly.

Trap 2: Tense mistakes

Be consistent with verb tense.

Trap 3: Overcomplication

Keep sentences clear and correct.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I write a structured response in 30 minutes?
  • Do I use varied vocabulary?
  • Are my verb conjugations accurate?
  • Can I speak confidently about key themes?
