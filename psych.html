Papers

  • Paper 1: SAQ/ERQ using core approaches + studies
  • Paper 2: option topic essays (depends on syllabus)
  • HL only Paper 3: research methods + stimulus-style
High scores come from: accurate study detail + linked evaluation.

High-impact study plan

  • Build a study bank: 2–3 studies per key concept
  • SAQ drills: short, tight answers with one strong study
  • ERQ practice: argument + counter + conclusion
  • Methods: sampling, bias, ethics, reliability/validity

Common mark traps

  • Describing the study but not answering the question
  • No link sentence (“this demonstrates… because…”)
  • Evaluation is generic (not tied to study design)
  • Forgetting ethics / bias in methods discussion
  • Vague conclusions (no judgement)

SAQ/ERQ mini templates

  • SAQ: Define → Explain → Study → Link back
  • ERQ: Thesis → Study 1 + eval → Study 2 + eval → Counter → Conclusion
