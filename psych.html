Subject Guide

Psychology

Psychology is a study + structure + evaluation subject. Marks come from organized answers and strong research knowledge.

High score rule: Always link theory to study, and evaluate clearly.

Assessment Structure

Exact structure varies by SL/HL and syllabus version.

Paper 1

Core topics.

  • Short answers (definitions + explanation).
  • Extended response (theory + study + evaluation).

Paper 2

Options.

  • Detailed study knowledge.
  • Strong evaluation paragraphs.

Paper 3 (HL)

Research methods.

  • Design questions.
  • Method evaluation.
  • Ethics + sampling.

Best Study Method (Psych)

  • Memorize key studies (aim, method, results, conclusion).
  • Practice ERQs with structured paragraphs.
  • Review research methods weekly.
  • Build evaluation sentence bank.
Psych mistake pattern: Writing too much description and not enough evaluation.

Extended Response Structure

  • Define theory.
  • Explain concept.
  • Describe study.
  • Evaluate (strengths + limitations).
  • Conclude with judgement.
Use linking phrases: “This suggests…”, “However…”, “One limitation is…”

Common Mark Traps (Psych)

Trap 1: Weak evaluation

Always include at least 2 strengths + 2 limitations.

Trap 2: Missing link to question

Answer the actual command term directly.

Trap 3: Forgetting research methods

Know sampling, ethics, reliability, validity.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I explain 3 core studies clearly?
  • Can I evaluate them effectively?
  • Do I understand research methods?
  • Can I structure a full ERQ in 40 minutes?
