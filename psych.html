Psychology

Psychology is study + structure + evaluation.

Theory → Study → Evaluation → Link to question.

Best Study Method

  • Memorize key studies clearly.
  • Practice ERQs weekly.
  • Build evaluation sentence bank.
  • Review research methods weekly.

Common Traps

  • No evaluation.
  • Weak command term response.
  • Forgetting sampling/ethics.
