Subject Guide

Economics

Economics is diagrams + explanation + evaluation.

High score rule: Define → Diagram → Explain → Evaluate (DEED).

Paper Structure

  • Paper 1: Essay questions.
  • Paper 2: Data response.
  • Paper 3 (HL): Quantitative policy calculations.

Scoring 7 Strategy

  • Always draw full diagrams.
  • Label axes + curves clearly.
  • Explain shifts step-by-step.
  • Include evaluation paragraphs.

Common Traps

  • Missing evaluation.
  • Unlabeled diagrams.
  • No real-world example.
