Chemistry

Paper structure + study strategy + common mark traps.

Papers

  • Paper 1A: MCQ
  • Paper 1B: data-based / skills
  • Paper 2: written responses + calculations + explanations
  • Paper 3: depends on syllabus/version (sometimes option-style)
Key idea: marks come from method + units + wording, not just final answers.

High-impact study plan

  • Phase 1: topic sets (easy → medium) with markscheme open
  • Phase 2: mixed sets (timed) to train switching topics
  • Daily: 10–15 min “weakest skill” drill (stoich, equilibrium, org, etc.)
  • Weekly: one timed mini-paper + fix errors
Build a one-page “mechanisms + conditions” sheet for organic (reactions, reagents, observations).

Common mark traps

  • Rounding too early → wrong final value
  • Unit conversion mistakes (cm³↔dm³, kPa↔Pa, g↔mol)
  • Skipping steps → losing method marks
  • Not stating assumptions/conditions (e.g., equilibrium shift logic)
  • Vague explanations (“it increases”) without naming the principle (IMFs, Le Châtelier, collision theory)

Quick checklist (before submitting)

  • Units shown and correct (and converted if needed)
  • Sig figs consistent with data
  • Working shown clearly (method marks)
  • Explanation uses correct keywords (IMF, oxidation state, limiting reagent, etc.)
  • Final answer boxed/obvious
