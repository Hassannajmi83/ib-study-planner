IB Grade Boundaries

Based on IB May 2025 (Zone A – Ontario reference). Use as a quick estimate — always verify with your school.

How to read this

Each line is the overall % range (final grade) that usually corresponds to each IB grade (1–7). These boundaries can shift by session and timezone, but this gives you a solid estimate.

Tip: Grade 6→7 jumps can be big — don’t rely on one test only.
