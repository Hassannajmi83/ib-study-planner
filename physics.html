Physics

Physics is a method-mark subject. Show every step.

Diagram → Formula → Substitute → Units → Sig figs.

Best Study Method

  • 4 problem sets per week.
  • Redo wrong questions after 24h.
  • Convert to SI immediately.
  • Always draw diagrams.

Common Traps

  • Forgetting unit conversion.
  • Missing direction/sign.
  • Skipping steps.
