Math AA

SL vs HL structure + accuracy + “show work” for method marks.

← Back to Subjects

Papers (typical)

  • Paper 1: non-calculator (algebra + exact values)
  • Paper 2: calculator (models, graphs, stats, numerics)
  • HL only: extra content depth + harder mixed reasoning
Biggest grade jump: do fewer questions, but review them perfectly.

High-impact study plan

  • Skill drills: short sets by topic (10–20 mins)
  • Mixed sets: timed “exam simulation” twice a week
  • Fix list: keep a running list of “I always mess up…”
  • Write clean solutions: teacher/IB wants readable logic

Common mark traps

  • Domain/range restrictions ignored
  • Trig answers not checked (sin/cos must be −1 to 1)
  • Dropping ± solutions
  • Rounding too early / calculator rounding
  • Missing reasoning lines → lost method marks

“Paper 1” checklist

  • Exact values (no decimals unless asked)
  • State restrictions (denominators, tan/cot, logs)
  • Show factoring steps (method marks)
  • Verify solutions back in original equation
