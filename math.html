Subject Guide

Mathematics AA

Math AA is a structure + precision subject. Marks come from showing working, respecting domain restrictions, and clean algebra.

High score rule: Every question must show logical flow. Even if calculator gives answer instantly, method earns marks.

Assessment (Typical Structure)

Exact details vary by SL/HL and syllabus year.

Paper 1

No calculator (usually).

  • Algebra discipline.
  • Exact values (π, surds).
  • Clear logical steps.

Paper 2

Calculator allowed.

  • Technology use (solve, integrate, matrices).
  • Interpretation of output.
  • Still show reasoning.

Paper 3 (HL)

Problem-solving.

  • Multi-step reasoning.
  • Model building.
  • Explain logic clearly.

Best Study Method (Math)

  • Daily: 6–10 mixed questions.
  • Weekly: 1 timed mini-paper.
  • Error log: track recurring algebra mistakes.
  • Redo wrong questions after 48 hours.
Math mistake pattern: skipping algebra steps causes sign/domain errors.

Answer Structure Template

  • 1) Write equation clearly.
  • 2) Show manipulation step-by-step.
  • 3) State restrictions (domain/denominator).
  • 4) Final answer boxed and labeled.
Always check: Is the trig value within [-1,1]? Did you state domain restrictions?

Common Mark Traps (Math)

Trap 1: Domain

Forgetting restrictions (log, denominator, trig).

Fix: always state domain explicitly.

Trap 2: Rounding

Rounding mid-solution.

Fix: round only at final answer.

Trap 3: Notation

Messy algebra loses clarity.

Fix: clean line spacing + logical structure.

Quick Practice Checklist

  • Can I solve without looking at worked solutions?
  • Did I show every algebra step?
  • Did I check domain restrictions?
  • Did I box final answers clearly?
